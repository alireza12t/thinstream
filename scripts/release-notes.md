Transcript loading fix.

**Fixed**

- The transcript often failed to load on some videos, mostly ones with only auto-generated captions. YouTube's player never filled its caption list, so Thinstream gave up. It now uses the caption list from the video's own data and loads the track.

Also includes the 1.0.2 phone and tablet fix.

**Install on Chrome, Edge, Brave or Arc**

1. Download `thinstream-chrome-1.0.3.zip` below and unzip it.
2. Open `chrome://extensions` and turn on Developer mode.
3. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder. If you loaded an older version, remove it first.

**Install on Firefox**

1. Download `thinstream-firefox-1.0.3.zip` below.
2. Open `about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox`.
3. Click Load Temporary Add-on and pick the zip. Needs Firefox 140 or newer.

**Safari**

The Mac app is in App Store review. The iPhone app is in TestFlight.
