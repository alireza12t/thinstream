Ad breaks in listen mode.

**New**

- During an ad, listen mode mutes it and shows a countdown to the end of the ad in place of the transcript. Sound and the transcript come back when the ad ends.
- A quiet jazz loop plays while the ad is muted, so the silence does not feel like a stall. It is made in the browser, nothing is downloaded, and you can switch it off on the ad screen.
- YouTube's own Skip button stays visible on top. Thinstream does not block or skip ads.

**Install on Chrome, Edge, Brave or Arc**

1. Download `thinstream-chrome-1.0.4.zip` below and unzip it.
2. Open `chrome://extensions` and turn on Developer mode.
3. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder. If you loaded an older version, remove it first.

**Install on Firefox**

1. Download `thinstream-firefox-1.0.4.zip` below.
2. Open `about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox`.
3. Click Load Temporary Add-on and pick the zip. Needs Firefox 140 or newer.
