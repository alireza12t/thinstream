Phone and tablet fix.

**Fixed**

- On phones and tablets the buttons in the transcript panel (language, text size, Show video) could not be tapped. The video player took the taps instead. On touch screens the panel is now a sheet at the bottom of the page, below the video. This applies in Safari, Chrome and Firefox.

Nothing changes on a computer.

**Install on Chrome, Edge, Brave or Arc**

1. Download `thinstream-chrome-1.0.2.zip` below and unzip it.
2. Open `chrome://extensions` and turn on Developer mode.
3. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder. If you loaded an older version, remove it first.

**Install on Firefox**

1. Download `thinstream-firefox-1.0.2.zip` below.
2. Open `about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox`.
3. Click Load Temporary Add-on and pick the zip. Needs Firefox 140 or newer.

**Safari**

The Mac app is in App Store review. The iPhone app is in TestFlight.
