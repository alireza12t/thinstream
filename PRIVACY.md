# Privacy

Thinstream collects nothing.

- It makes no network requests of its own. There is no server, no analytics and no account.
- It runs only on `youtube.com`.
- It reads two things from the YouTube page you are on: the list of video formats with their sizes, and the caption track the YouTube player loads. Both stay in the page. They are used to show the transcript and the data estimate.
- It stores a few settings in your browser's local extension storage: whether listen mode is on, text size, caption language, the "hide recommendations" switch, and a running estimate of time listened and data saved. This never leaves your browser.
- It keeps two small notes in YouTube's local storage while listen mode is on, so it can put your video quality and caption setting back when you turn it off.

Removing the extension removes its stored settings.
