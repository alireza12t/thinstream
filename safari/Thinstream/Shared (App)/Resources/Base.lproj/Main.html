Thinstream adds a Listen mode button to YouTube in Safari. One tap holds the video at 144p, hides it, and shows a transcript that follows the audio. Made for slow internet.

To turn it on: open Settings, Apps, Safari, Extensions, Thinstream, and allow it. Then open a video on youtube.com in Safari and allow Thinstream on that site.

After you turn it on, open a video on youtube.com and allow Thinstream on that site. The button sits in the player controls, next to captions.
